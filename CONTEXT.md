# Brittany 2026 Family Guide

A private guide for one booked family trip to Brittany, 6–23 August 2026. It began as a decision
guide — which region, which base, which dates — and is now a trip companion: given where the family
is and what the day looks like, it offers options, each traceable to the research it came from.

## Language

### The trip

**Stay**:
One booked accommodation: a place plus its check-in and check-out dates. A stay owns the nights
between those dates, so a moving day belongs to the stay slept in that evening. The same place can
be two stays — Nantes is one at the start of the trip and another before the flight home.
_Avoid_: leg, hotel, stop, accommodation

**Base**:
One of the six candidate regions the pre-booking comparison scored. A base is an argument, not a
location we necessarily sleep in: three of the six are on the trip, three are not, and Nantes is a
stay without ever being a base.
_Avoid_: destination, region, area

**Day conditions**:
The two facts the family supplies about a given day — the weather and how much time is free. They
are the only inputs that reorder the options; opening hours and travel fatigue were deliberately
excluded.
_Avoid_: filters, preferences, context

**Reach**:
The relation between a stay and a place, tagged either *nearby* or *day trip*. Reach is curated by
hand and is not the same as which base a place sits in — places belonging to bases we never sleep in
are still reachable from the stays we booked.
_Avoid_: distance, proximity, radius

### Places and evidence

**Place**:
Somewhere the family can go and spend time. Places are reachable from stays and carry the fields the
day conditions act on.
_Avoid_: attraction, activity, POI, thing to do

**Source document**:
An original research report, stored unchanged and shown in its own language. Never edited, never
translated wholesale.
_Avoid_: research, report, doc

**Source block**:
A reviewable slice of a source document. Every substantive block must carry exactly one explicit
decision: retained, duplicate, or conflict.
_Avoid_: chunk, section, excerpt

**Evidence**:
A concise English claim written from one or more agreeing source blocks, carrying its kind, its
qualifiers and — when the claim can go stale — the date it was last checked.
_Avoid_: fact, citation, note

**Freshness**:
Whether an evidence record's check date still falls inside its review window. A stale record is
labelled, never hidden, and never fails the build.
_Avoid_: staleness, expiry, TTL

### Places that are easily confused

**Dinan**:
The walled medieval town inland on the Rance, part of the Saint-Malo / Dinan base and the fourth
stay of the trip.

**Dinard**:
The seaside resort facing Saint-Malo across the estuary. Not a stay, not a base, and not where the
family sleeps — the similarity to Dinan is the trap.

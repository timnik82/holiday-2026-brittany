## Methodology

<!-- paragraph id="method-1" sources="" -->
This guide is built from primary research into Brittany as a family holiday destination for August 2026. Every recommendation is based on verified, publicly available information from official tourism sites, transport operators, and local authorities.

<!-- paragraph id="method-2" sources="" -->
We use a structured content pipeline that validates every paragraph against its sources, ensuring that no claim appears without evidence to support it.

## Ranking transparency

<!-- paragraph id="ranking-1" sources="" -->
Destinations and activities are ranked by a transparent scoring system that weighs family-friendliness, accessibility, cost, and seasonal availability equally.

<!-- paragraph id="ranking-2" sources="" -->
No destination receives preferential placement. The scoring criteria are documented and applied consistently across all entries in the guide.

## Source preservation

<!-- paragraph id="sources-1" sources="" -->
Every factual claim in this guide links back to its original source through our citation system. Sources are archived and timestamped to protect against link rot.

<!-- paragraph id="sources-2" sources="" -->
When a source becomes unavailable, we flag the affected content for review and seek replacement evidence before the next publication cycle.

## Freshness labels

<!-- paragraph id="fresh-1" sources="" -->
Each page carries an updated-at date and a status label indicating whether content is in draft, under review, or published. This helps readers gauge how current the information is.

<!-- paragraph id="fresh-2" sources="" -->
We aim to verify all published content within thirty days of the travel date to ensure recommendations remain accurate and actionable.

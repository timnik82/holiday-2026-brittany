## The family budget

<!-- paragraph id="plan-accommodation-budget" sources="evidence:practical-accommodation-budget-overview" -->
For a family of two adults and one child, realistic August accommodation is roughly €100 to €160 per night for a good family apartment or gîte booked early, and €140 to €220 per night for a 3 to 4-star hotel. The family's planning target is €150 per night, with a ceiling of €180. Brittany offers over 79,000 rental properties, but August is peak season — the famous coastal resorts sit at the top of the range.

## Per-base ranges

<!-- paragraph id="plan-accommodation-per-base" sources="evidence:northern-saint-malo-accommodation-high,evidence:northern-granit-rose-accommodation,evidence:western-brest-accommodation,evidence:western-quimper-accommodation,evidence:western-crozon-accommodation,evidence:southern-accommodation-pressure" -->
Indicative August nightly ranges by base (apartments / family hotels):

- [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan) — apartments €130 to €300, family hotels €120 to €260. Sources disagree on price; budget toward the higher range inside the walls or near the Sillon.
- [Côte de Granit Rose](/bases/cote-de-granit-rose) — apartments €120 to €220, family hotels €110 to €260. Somewhat cheaper than Saint-Malo; sea-view properties carry a steep premium.
- [Brest / Finistère](/bases/brest-finistere) — apartments €70 to €150, family hotels €80 to €180. Best value of the shortlist; a 3-star family room around €70 to €90.
- [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere) — apartments €80 to €160, family hotels €90 to €170. Gentle budget option; some aggregators show August dipping below June.
- [Crozon / Douarnenez](/bases/crozon-douarnenez) — apartments €100 to €220, family hotels €120 to €220. Between Brest value and coastal-resort pricing.
- [Vannes / Carnac / Morbihan](/bases/vannes-carnac-morbihan) — apartments €130 to €260, family hotels €110 to €240. Most expensive after Saint-Malo, especially by the sea.

## Accommodation types

<!-- paragraph id="plan-accommodation-types" sources="evidence:practical-accommodation-types" -->
Brittany's accommodation spans camping (including 4 to 5-star family resorts like La Grande Métairie in Carnac, with pools and kids' clubs), gîtes and self-catering apartments, family hotels, and resort residences. For a family with a child, the campsites and apartment/gîte options tend to give the best space-to-price ratio, while old-town hotels trade space for walkability.

## Book early

<!-- paragraph id="plan-accommodation-book-early" sources="evidence:practical-accommodation-book-early" -->
August is peak season and the most sought-after family properties — sea-view apartments, walkable old-town hotels, 4 to 5-star family campsites — book out months ahead. Lock in accommodation before flights, especially for Saint-Malo, Carnac and the Côte de Granit Rose.

## Why food matters here

<!-- paragraph id="plan-food-overview" sources="evidence:practical-food-overview" -->
Food is a clear strength of Brittany and carries the same 15% ranking weight as accommodation. The family-friendly staples — crêpes and savoury galettes (buckwheat), moules-frites, fresh seafood and oysters, cider, and Breton butter biscuits (kouign-amann, palets) — make family meals easy, distinctive and affordable across every base. Markets, harbour stalls and crêperies are everywhere; children's menus and simple dishes are widely available.

## Regional highlights

<!-- paragraph id="plan-food-regional" sources="evidence:practical-food-regional-highlights" -->
Each base has a food signature worth planning a meal around:

- [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan) — [Cancale](/things-to-do/cancale) oysters at the harbour stalls, and [Dinan](/things-to-do/dinan)'s Thursday market.
- [Côte de Granit Rose](/bases/cote-de-granit-rose) — crêperies and fresh seafood around Perros-Guirec and Trégastel.
- [Brest / Finistère](/bases/brest-finistere) — port seafood and the [Concarneau](/things-to-do/concarneau) walled-town fish market.
- [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere) — [Quimper](/things-to-do/quimper) crêperies, [Pont-Aven](/things-to-do/pont-aven) almond biscuits, and [Locronan](/things-to-do/locronan).
- [Crozon / Douarnenez](/bases/crozon-douarnenez) — harbour seafood at [Morgat](/things-to-do/morgat) and Camaret.
- [Vannes / Carnac / Morbihan](/bases/vannes-carnac-morbihan) — [Carnac](/things-to-do/carnac-alignments) and [Quiberon](/things-to-do/quiberon) oysters, [Vannes](/things-to-do/vannes) market on Place des Lices, and [Auray / Saint-Goustan](/things-to-do/auray-saint-goustan).

## For a child

<!-- paragraph id="plan-food-child" sources="evidence:practical-food-overview" -->
For a child, galettes (savoury buckwheat crêpes), sweet crêpes, moules-frites, and simple fish dishes are easy to find everywhere and need no special ordering. Cider comes in both alcoholic and non-alcoholic forms; Breton biscuits (kouign-amann, palets bretons, sablés) make good souvenirs and snacks.

## The car recommendation

<!-- paragraph id="plan-getting-around-car" sources="evidence:practical-car-tradeoffs,evidence:routes-car-required-flexible" -->
Across every base, a rental car picked up at the gateway airport is the single biggest enabler of a flexible family trip. The old cities — [Saint-Malo](/bases/saint-malo-dinan), [Vannes](/bases/vannes-carnac-morbihan), [Quimper](/bases/quimper-south-finistere) and [Brest](/bases/brest-finistere) — are walkable and rail-connected, but beaches, megalith sites, capes, island ferries and the best viewpoints are dispersed along local roads that trains and BreizhGo buses do not reliably cover.

## Where a car matters most and least

<!-- paragraph id="plan-getting-around-by-base" sources="evidence:practical-car-tradeoffs,evidence:western-crozon-logistics-car" -->
The car-requirement rises with how dispersed a base's best sights are. [Crozon / Douarnenez](/bases/crozon-douarnenez) is effectively car-essential — its point is driving between dispersed viewpoints. The [Côte de Granit Rose](/bases/cote-de-granit-rose) is car-recommended for the same reason. Saint-Malo, Vannes, Quimper and Brest can work car-free if you stay in the old town and use trains plus the occasional taxi or organised tour for a signature day trip.

## The trade-offs

<!-- paragraph id="plan-getting-around-tradeoffs" sources="evidence:practical-car-tradeoffs" -->
The trade-off is parking and toll costs (budget roughly €300 to €400 for a week's fuel and tolls combined) versus reach. For a single-base trip a car is recommended; for Crozon it is essential; for a purely urban stay in Saint-Malo or Brest it is helpful but not mandatory. Pick the car up at the gateway airport rather than in-city to avoid a transit-heavy arrival day.

## Driving your own car

<!-- paragraph id="plan-getting-around-own-car" sources="evidence:routes-own-car-impractical,evidence:practical-transport-two-scenarios" -->
Driving the family's own car from Portugal is a 1,600 to 1,900 km, 15 to 19 hour commitment one way, best split across two days with an overnight stop near Bordeaux or Biarritz. It only makes sense when you are combining Brittany with an extended road tour; as a pure transfer, flying plus a local rental is lighter and cheaper.

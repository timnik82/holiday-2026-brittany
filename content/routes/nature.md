## Why this route

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-overview" sources="evidence:routes-nature-overview" -->
This route is built on the [Côte de Granit Rose](/bases/cote-de-granit-rose), the strongest nature base in Brittany, and extends to the [Crozon / Douarnenez](/bases/crozon-douarnenez) peninsula for a wilder oceanic second leg. It is pitched at families who want every day to be visually striking but without exhausting drives — pink-granite geology, the GR34 customs path, seabird boat trips and a major ocean-science aquarium, with refreshing sea and fresh air throughout.

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-fit" sources="evidence:routes-nature-overview,evidence:routes-nature-stay-lengths" -->
Choose this over the [cultural](/routes/cultural) or [relaxed](/routes/relaxed-family) routes when scenery, wildlife and short walks matter more than old towns or warm swimming. The sea here is refreshing rather than warm — suited to short swims and shallow play, not long bathing.

## How to get here

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-entry" sources="evidence:routes-entry-airports,evidence:routes-nature-transfers" -->
The most efficient entry is a direct Porto–Brest flight, then the train Brest–Lannion from 1 h 15 min onto the north coast. The alternative is to fly into Nantes and drive north to the Côte de Granit Rose — about 260 km on day one, a longer first-day transfer that argues for an early-morning arrival. Direct Porto–Brest flights are seasonal, so confirm them before planning around them.

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-car" sources="evidence:routes-car-required-flexible" -->
A rental car is recommended. The granitrose villages and Crozon coves are scattered along local roads, and public transport does not reliably reach the best beaches, trails and headlands. Pick one up at Brest or Nantes airport.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 — Arrival on the Côte de Granit Rose

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-1" sources="evidence:routes-nature-transfers,evidence:routes-nature-stay-lengths" -->
Arrive via Brest (Porto direct flight) or Nantes (longer drive north), settle into a base around Perros-Guirec or Trégastel. Walk a first stretch of coast in the evening. If you entered via Nantes, treat today mainly as a travel day.

### Day 2 — Ploumanac'h and the GR34

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-2" sources="evidence:routes-nature-stay-lengths" -->
The signature day: the [GR34 customs path at Ploumanac'h](/things-to-do/ploumanach) and the pink granite boulders. Children love scrambling over the giant pink rocks and spotting animal shapes in them. Short beach stretch in the afternoon.

### Day 3 — Sept-Îles bird reserve

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-3" sources="evidence:routes-nature-stay-lengths" -->
A [boat trip to the Sept-Îles archipelago](/things-to-do/sept-iles) for Atlantic puffins and seabirds. Weather alternative if boats are cancelled: a family beach day in Trégastel or Perros-Guirec.

### Day 4 — Parc du Radôme

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-4" sources="evidence:routes-nature-stay-lengths" -->
A [rainy-day anchor at the Parc du Radôme](/things-to-do/parc-du-radome) — the Cité des Télécoms and planetarium. This is the natural weather swap for day 2 or 3.

### Day 5 — Paimpol and Bréhat

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-5" sources="evidence:routes-nature-stay-lengths" -->
A gentler linked-area day to [Paimpol and the Île de Bréhat](/things-to-do/paimpol-brehat) — a car-free island with rented bikes and gardens. Weather alternative: a final granitrose beach day closer to base.

### Day 6 — Move south to Crozon

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-6" sources="evidence:routes-nature-sequencing,evidence:routes-nature-transfers" -->
The single travel day: move south to the [Crozon peninsula](/bases/crozon-douarnenez), roughly 4 days per region as the sources suggest. Settle in and walk a first headland in the evening.

### Day 7 — Pen-Hir and Cap de la Chèvre

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-7" sources="evidence:routes-nature-sequencing" -->
A clifftop day at [Pen-Hir and the Cap de la Chèvre](/things-to-do/crozon-pen-hir) — wild heather, sea stacks and the strongest oceanic feel of the trip. Sturdy shoes and a wind layer; the trails are exposed.

### Day 8 — Morgat beach and sheltered swim

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-8" sources="evidence:routes-nature-overview" -->
A calmer day at [Morgat beach and bay](/things-to-do/morgat), the sheltered swim spot on the peninsula. Weather alternative: the [Maison des Minéraux](/things-to-do/maison-des-mineraux) for family geology.

### Day 9 — Océanopolis (Brest)

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-9" sources="evidence:routes-nature-sequencing" -->
A day trip from the Crozon base to [Océanopolis in Brest](/things-to-do/oceanopolis) — one of the largest oceanariums in Europe, the trip's main science attraction. Re-check opening days and ticket prices on the official site before visiting.

### Day 10 — Exit

<!-- paragraph id="routes-nature-day-10" sources="evidence:routes-nature-transfers,evidence:routes-entry-airports" -->
Exit via Brest (if the Porto direct flight suits) or train/drive back to Nantes. Keep the last morning unhurried and close to your exit airport.

## Why this route

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-overview" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-overview" -->
This route is built on the [Vannes / Carnac / Morbihan](/bases/vannes-carnac-morbihan) base. The sources describe it as the most even, lowest-stress option in Brittany — the warmest and calmest bathing water, sandy beaches, island boats, and a slower pace. It is the route that suits a child who tires on long transfers, and it needs no internal travel day.

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-fit" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-overview,evidence:routes-relaxed-pace" -->
Choose this over the [cultural](/routes/cultural) or [nature](/routes/nature) routes when warm swimming and a calm rhythm matter more than old towns or wild coast. The route is explicitly built to stretch without becoming a race — it can easily extend to 9 or 10 days by adding beach and island days.

## How to get here

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-entry" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-transfers,evidence:routes-entry-airports" -->
This is the lightest first-day transfer of the three routes. Fly into Nantes, shuttle to Gare Sud, then the train Nantes–Vannes from 1 h 12 min — described by the sources as one of the softest door-to-destination scenarios in all of Brittany. By car, Nantes to Carnac is about 135 km and Nantes to the Morbihan coast around 1.5 hours. Train schedules are seasonal — re-check times before booking.

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-car" sources="evidence:routes-car-required-flexible" -->
A rental car is recommended rather than essential. Vannes and Carnac themselves are walkable and well connected by train, but the megalith sites, Suscinio castle, the Quiberon peninsula and the island ferries are far easier with a car. Pick one up at Nantes airport.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 — Arrival in Vannes

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-1" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-transfers,evidence:routes-relaxed-stay-lengths" -->
Arrive in Nantes, train to Vannes, settle in. Walk the [medieval old town, walls and port](/things-to-do/vannes), and the market on Place des Lices if your timing suits. No long transfer today.

### Day 2 — Île-aux-Moines or Île d'Arz

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-2" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-stay-lengths" -->
A boat to [Île-aux-Moines](/things-to-do/ile-aux-moines) or [Île d'Arz](/things-to-do/ile-d-arz) for rented bikes, quiet lanes and small coves. Weather alternative: a sheltered gulf beach day and a crêperie evening in Vannes.

### Day 3 — Suscinio and the Rhuys peninsula

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-3" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-stay-lengths" -->
A day trip by car to [Château de Suscinio](/things-to-do/suscinio) and the beaches of the Rhuys peninsula — a medieval castle on the gulf with sandy swimming nearby.

### Day 4 — Move (or stay) to Carnac

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-4" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-sequencing,evidence:routes-relaxed-stay-lengths" -->
A short move to [Carnac / La Trinité-sur-Mer](/things-to-do/carnac-alignments), or stay put on the gulf and treat Carnac as day trips. This is the only "move" of the route and it is optional — the whole trip can run from a single Morbihan base.

### Day 5 — Carnac megaliths and Grande Plage

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-5" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-stay-lengths" -->
Morning at the [Carnac alignments and Maison des Mégalithes](/things-to-do/carnac-alignments), afternoon on the Grande Plage. Weather alternative: the [Parc de Branféré](/things-to-do/branfere), a family eco-park and animal reserve nearby.

### Day 6 — Quiberon peninsula

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-6" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-stay-lengths" -->
A day trip to the [Quiberon peninsula](/things-to-do/quiberon) — wild coast on the ocean side, sheltered coves on the bay side. Weather alternative: an oyster lunch at [Auray / Saint-Goustan](/things-to-do/auray-saint-goustan) and a short market walk.

### Day 7 — Weather reserve / optional inland extension

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-7" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-inland-extension,evidence:routes-relaxed-pace" -->
Keep this day as a weather reserve — an extra beach, an oyster lunch for the adults, a crêperie and a short market stop. For families who want an inland contrast, the optional extension runs to the [Brocéliande forest (Paimpont)](/things-to-do/broceliande) — legendary forest of Merlin, with the Merlin-themed La Porte des Secrets — and [Lac de Trémelin](/things-to-do/lac-de-tremelin), a freshwater swim. This inland leg has thinner direct support than the coastal days; treat it as an optional, lower-confidence extension.

### Day 8 — Exit via Nantes

<!-- paragraph id="routes-relaxed-day-8" sources="evidence:routes-relaxed-transfers,evidence:routes-entry-airports" -->
Train or drive back to Nantes for the flight home. The return leg is as short as the arrival — about 1 h 12 min by train from Vannes.

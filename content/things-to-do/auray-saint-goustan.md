## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="auray-saint-goustan-what" sources="evidence:southern-auray-saint-goustan" -->
Auray and its old port of Saint-Goustan make a small historic stop that pairs well with the gulf route — a harbour of cobbled streets and timbered houses reached as a short detour from Carnac or Vannes.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 3 hours; combine with Vannes or a gulf island trip.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the harbour and old streets suit a relaxed family stroll.
- **Weather fit:** mostly outdoor; best in fair weather.
- **Booking:** none for the town; waterside restaurants may need booking in August.
- **Transport:** a short drive from Carnac or Vannes; a car is helpful.
- **Safety:** standard town supervision.

**Base:** [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="bon-secours-what" sources="evidence:northern-bon-secours-tidal-pool" -->
Bon-Secours is the family beach inside Saint-Malo, with a unique seawater tidal pool and diving tower set against the city walls. It is the easy swimming spot when you are already in the old town.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 1 to 3 hours.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the tidal pool suits confident young swimmers.
- **Weather fit:** a fine-weather highlight; usable in overcast weather if wind is not strong.
- **Booking:** none — public beach and pool.
- **Transport:** a short walk from anywhere in Intra-Muros.
- **Safety:** the water is refreshing (around 18 degrees Celsius in August); supervise children at the diving tower. The nearby long beach, Plage du Sillon, is fully exposed to large tides — check tide times before settling there.

**Inland linked place** — part of the relaxed route, not part of the coastal base.

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="broceliande-what" sources="evidence:southern-broceliande" -->
The Brocéliande forest, around Paimpont inland of Vannes, is the legendary forest of Merlin and the fairy Vivian. Its main family stop is La Porte des Secrets, an interactive show-space at Paimpont that tells the Arthurian legends of Merlin, Vivian and the Knights of the Round Table. It is an inland, myth-and-forest contrast to the coastal south and a natural fit for the relaxed family route.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day to a full day around Paimpont.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the legends and forest walks suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** forest walks suit most weather; La Porte des Secrets is indoor.
- **Booking:** check the official site for current show times before visiting.
- **Transport:** inland from Vannes — a car is recommended.
- **Safety:** standard forest-walk supervision.
- **Price / checked date:** opening days and show times change — re-check on the official site before visiting (last reviewed against the supplied research on 2026-07-02).

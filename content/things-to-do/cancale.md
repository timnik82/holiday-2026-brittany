**Base:** [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="cancale-what" sources="evidence:northern-cancale-oysters,evidence:northern-cancale-oyster-market-detail" -->
Cancale, about 25–30 minutes from Saint-Malo, is the area's oyster capital: the Marché aux huîtres by the Houle lighthouse sells freshly opened oysters from the growers to eat on the seawall benches overlooking the bay.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="cancale-planning" sources="evidence:northern-cancale-oyster-market-detail,evidence:northern-distances-msm-cluster" -->
Arrive early in summer — free port parking fills fast. A dozen runs about €5.50–7.50. Pointe du Grouin adds about an hour of coastal views if you want more than the stalls.

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 4 hours.
- **Age fit:** all ages; oysters are for the curious, crêperies and simple seafood cover the rest.
- **Weather fit:** a seafront walk best in fair weather; the harbour stalls are open-air.
- **Booking:** none for the market stalls; sit-down restaurants may need booking in August.
- **Transport:** about 25–30 minutes by car from Saint-Malo; bus possible but the car is easier.
- **Safety:** standard seafront supervision.

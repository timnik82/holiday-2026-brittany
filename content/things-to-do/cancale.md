**Base:** [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="cancale-what" sources="evidence:northern-cancale-oysters" -->
Cancale, a short trip from Saint-Malo, is the area's oyster capital: the harbour-side stalls (marché à l'huître) sell freshly opened oysters to eat right on the seawall. It pairs seafood with a relaxed harbour walk and is an easy food-focused half-day trip.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 4 hours.
- **Age fit:** all ages; oysters are for the curious, crêperies and simple seafood cover the rest.
- **Weather fit:** a seafront walk best in fair weather; the harbour stalls are open-air.
- **Booking:** none for the market stalls; sit-down restaurants may need booking in August.
- **Transport:** short drive or bus from Saint-Malo; car-optional but easier by car.
- **Safety:** standard seafront supervision.

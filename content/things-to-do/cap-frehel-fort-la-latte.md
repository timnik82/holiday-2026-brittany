**Base:** [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="cap-frehel-what" sources="evidence:northern-cap-frehel-fort-la-latte" -->
Cap Fréhel and the neighbouring Fort La Latte are a classic day trip from Saint-Malo by car: dramatic clifftop heath, a fortified headland castle, and wide coastal views. It is a primary reason a car is described as helpful for this base even though the town itself is walkable.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a full day, or a long half-day combined with Dinan.
- **Age fit:** 6+; the castle and cliff paths suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** exposed — best on a calm, dry day; avoid high winds on the headland.
- **Booking:** none for the headland; Fort La Latte may charge entry — check opening days.
- **Transport:** most practical by car; public transport to the headland is limited.
- **Safety:** unfenced cliff edges in places — keep children on marked paths.

**Base:** [Vannes / Carnac / Morbihan](/bases/vannes-carnac-morbihan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="carnac-alignments-what" sources="evidence:southern-carnac-megaliths" -->
Carnac is famous for its megalithic alignements — thousands of menhirs in long rows, the largest such complex in the world, dated to roughly 4600 to 4300 BC, plus the Maison des Mégalithes visitor centre. Close access to the stones is by paid guided tour in season, but the tourist centre shows short video presentations that suit a restless child.

## Beach nearby

<!-- paragraph id="carnac-alignments-beach" sources="evidence:southern-carnac-beach-detail,evidence:southern-protected-water-beaches" -->
After the stones, Grande Plage is several kilometres of fine sand with a gentle slope and children's clubs in season; Kervillen nearby is a sheltered family gem at the entrance to Quiberon Bay. Lifeguards run in July–August on this calm Morbihan side.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 3 hours across the centre and a guided row; add beach time separately.
- **Age fit:** 6+; the scale and the visitor-centre videos suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** mostly outdoor; the visitor centre offers cover. For a true indoor morning use the [Musée de Préhistoire](/things-to-do/musee-prehistoire-carnac).
- **Booking:** close access is by paid guided tour in season — book ahead in August.
- **Transport:** on-site in Carnac; a car helps if staying outside the town.
- **Safety:** stay on marked paths and behind the stones' protective fences.
- **Price / checked date:** tour and visitor-centre schedules change — re-check before visiting (last reviewed 2026-07-25).

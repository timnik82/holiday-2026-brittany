**Base:** [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="chateau-de-dinan-what" sources="evidence:northern-chateau-de-dinan,evidence:northern-dinan-medieval-town" -->
The Château de Dinan is the town's 14th-century keep, now running modern scenography on warfare and court life with hands-on facsimiles aimed at ages 6–12. It is the indoor half of a Dinan day when the half-timbered streets turn wet.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="chateau-de-dinan-planning" sources="evidence:northern-chateau-de-dinan" -->
Allow 45–60 minutes indoors, then continue outdoors to rue du Jerzual and the port if the weather holds.

- **Visit duration:** 45 to 60 minutes.
- **Age fit:** 6+; strongest for a child who likes castles and hands-on displays.
- **Weather fit:** fully indoor — the local rain fallback on a Dinan day.
- **Booking:** walk-up usually fine; family pass (2+2) about €21.
- **Tickets:** adults ~€8, concessions €5, under-6 free.
- **Hours:** July–August about 10:30–19:00.
- **Transport:** in Dinan town; combine with the medieval streets on the same day.
- **Safety:** stairs and medieval floors — standard castle supervision.

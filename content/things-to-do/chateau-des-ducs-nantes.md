## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="nantes-chateau-what" sources="evidence:nantes-chateau-overview" -->
Château des ducs de Bretagne works on two levels: the courtyard, moat gardens and ramparts are free and give the castle feel a child wants, while the history museum inside adds depth without being essential on a tired day. A family discovery kit helps keep a ten-year-old engaged. Summer 2026 Voyage work Notre dit pays sits in the moats.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** about 90 minutes for ramparts and courtyards only; about two hours with a concise museum visit.
- **Age fit:** 6+; rampart views and the kit suit a ten-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** ramparts and moat are outdoor; museum and rampart towers offer cover in rain.
- **Booking:** no booking for ramparts; museum tickets at the desk or online in peak season.
- **Transport:** walkable from Bouffay and Commerce; pairs with the Water Mirror opposite.
- **Safety:** rampart walls and moat edges need supervision.
- **Price / checked date:** museum about €9 adult, free under 18; ramparts free (last reviewed 2026-07-25).

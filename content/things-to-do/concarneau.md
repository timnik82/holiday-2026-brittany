**Base:** [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="concarneau-what" sources="evidence:western-concarneau-filets-bleus,evidence:western-filets-bleus-practical" -->
Concarneau's walled Ville Close, a 14th-century sea fortress reached by bridge, is the town's signature sight. In August 2026 the Festival des Filets Bleus runs Wednesday 12 to Sunday 16 August — free Quai Carnot concerts, a Village de la Mer, and a Sunday Grande Parade with more than 2,000 costumed participants.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="concarneau-planning" sources="evidence:western-filets-bleus-practical,evidence:western-concarneau-filets-bleus" -->
During the festival park at the station or shuttle car parks and use Coralie buses (lines 1 and 4). The Sunday parade starts about 10:00; a support badge is roughly €5–10 and free for under-10s. Concerts on Quai Carnot stay free.

- **Visit duration:** a half-day for the Ville Close; a full evening or day during the festival.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the parade, music and maritime village suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** concerts are open-air; the Ville Close walls offer some cover.
- **Booking:** parade badges and lodging sell out — book ahead for mid-August.
- **Transport:** about 30 minutes by car from Quimper; avoid driving into the centre on festival days.
- **Safety:** large crowds — keep children close and agree a meeting point.
- **Price / checked date:** dates confirmed 12–16 August 2026; badge prices can shift (last reviewed 2026-07-25).

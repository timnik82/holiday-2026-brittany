**Base:** [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="concarneau-what" sources="evidence:western-concarneau-filets-bleus" -->
Concarneau's walled Ville Close, a 14th-century sea fortress reached by bridge, is the town's signature sight. In August 2026 the Festival des Filets Bleus runs from Wednesday 12 to Sunday 16 August — five days of free open-air concerts on the Quai Carnot facing the ramparts, a Grande Parade on the Sunday with more than 2,000 costumed participants, and a Village de la Mer celebrating fishing and maritime life. The official festival site confirms the dates.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day for the Ville Close; a full evening or day during the festival.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the parade, music, and maritime village suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** concerts are open-air — check the forecast; the Ville Close walls offer some cover.
- **Booking:** the Grande Parade is ticketed (around €10, free for under-10s); book concerts and accommodation well ahead — August is busy.
- **Transport:** about 30 minutes by car from Quimper; a car is recommended.
- **Safety:** large festival crowds in August — keep children close and agree a meeting point.
- **Price / checked date:** festival dates confirmed for 2026 (12 to 16 August) but specifics can shift — re-check the official site closer to the date (official check 2026-07-02).

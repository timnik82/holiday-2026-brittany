**Base:** [Crozon / Douarnenez](/bases/crozon-douarnenez)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="crozon-pen-hir-what" sources="evidence:western-crozon-wild-coast,evidence:western-crozon-pen-hir-detail" -->
Pointe de Pen-Hir rises about 63 m above the sea with the distinctive Tas de Pois rock stacks — the strongest end-of-the-land viewpoint on a Quimper-based Crozon day. Cap de la Chèvre and the GR34 link further heath, granite and open ocean.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="crozon-pen-hir-planning" sources="evidence:western-crozon-pen-hir-detail,evidence:western-crozon-logistics-car" -->
Viewpoints are dispersed along local roads, so a car is essential from Quimper. Pack wind layers even in August; on a wet day swap to the [Maison des Minéraux](/things-to-do/maison-des-mineraux) or drive on to [Océanopolis](/things-to-do/oceanopolis).

- **Visit duration:** a half-day to a full day between viewpoints.
- **Age fit:** 6+; cliff paths suit a 9 to 10-year-old with supervision.
- **Weather fit:** exposed — best on a calm, dry day.
- **Booking:** none for the coastal path.
- **Transport:** by car from Quimper; trains and buses do not cover the viewpoints well.
- **Safety:** unfenced cliff edges — keep children on marked paths.

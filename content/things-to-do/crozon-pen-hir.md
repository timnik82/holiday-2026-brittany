## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="crozon-pen-hir-what" sources="evidence:western-crozon-wild-coast" -->
The signature outdoor days on the Crozon peninsula run between dispersed viewpoints: the clifftop heath and rocks of Pen-Hir, the wild paths of the Cap de la Chèvre, and the GR34 trail linking coves and headlands. The area is the shortlist's strongest pick for genuine wild Brittany — rocks, capes, geology, open ocean, and a strong end-of-the-land feel.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day to a full day, walking between viewpoints.
- **Age fit:** 6+; cliff paths and rock scrambling suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** exposed — best on a calm, dry day; avoid high winds on the headlands.
- **Booking:** none for the coastal path.
- **Transport:** most practical by car; viewpoints are dispersed along local roads.
- **Safety:** unfenced cliff edges in places — keep children on marked paths; supervise scrambling.

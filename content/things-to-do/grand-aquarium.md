**Base:** [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="grand-aquarium-what" sources="evidence:northern-saint-malo-grand-aquarium" -->
The Grand Aquarium de Saint-Malo is consistently named the area's leading family attraction, with a submarine-style descent and hands-on marine contact. It is the base's main rainy-day anchor.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 4 hours.
- **Age fit:** 4+; strong for a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** fully indoor — the best rainy-day option in the base.
- **Booking:** check the official site for current opening times and ticket prices; book ahead in peak August weeks.
- **Transport:** short drive or bus from the city centre; car-optional.
- **Safety:** standard indoor attraction supervision.
- **Price / checked date:** prices change — re-check on the official site before visiting (last reviewed against the supplied research on 2026-06-30).

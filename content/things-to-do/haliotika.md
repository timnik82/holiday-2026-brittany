**Base:** [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="haliotika-what" sources="evidence:western-haliotika" -->
At the port of Le Guilvinec, the Haliotika fishing discovery centre runs interactive programmes for children, including a tour of the trawler Bara Breizh and a look at a working fish auction. It is a hands-on option that fits a child curious about how fish reach the table.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 3 hours.
- **Age fit:** 5+; good for a curious 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** partly indoor; a useful wet-weather or port-side stop.
- **Booking:** check the official site for current tour times and auction-viewing hours before visiting.
- **Transport:** within day-trip range of Quimper; a car is recommended.
- **Safety:** standard attraction and quayside supervision.
- **Price / checked date:** tour times and auction hours change — re-check on the official site before visiting (last reviewed against the supplied research on 2026-07-02).

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="ile-aux-moines-what" sources="evidence:southern-islands-gulf,evidence:southern-islands-ferry-prices" -->
The Île-aux-Moines is the largest island in the Morbihan gulf, reached by a short passenger ferry and explored by bicycle and on foot — calm beaches and a relaxed pace make it a classic family day trip alongside traditional gulf cruises such as the wooden schooner Le Corbeau des mers.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="ile-aux-moines-planning" sources="evidence:southern-islands-ferry-prices,evidence:southern-islands-gulf" -->
Passeur des Îles crossings start from about €8 return; longer mini-cruises cost more. Pair one morning ferry with bike hire rather than trying both gulf islands in a single day. If the weather closes in, swap to Vannes old town or the Carnac museum.

- **Visit duration:** a full day.
- **Age fit:** all ages; cycling and beach play suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** best in fair weather; the crossing is sea-condition dependent.
- **Booking:** ferries are seasonal and weather-dependent — check times and book ahead in August.
- **Transport:** passenger ferry from the gulf ports; the island itself is car-free.
- **Safety:** supervise children on the ferry and around the water.
- **Price / checked date:** ferry tariffs change — re-check before travel (last reviewed 2026-07-25).

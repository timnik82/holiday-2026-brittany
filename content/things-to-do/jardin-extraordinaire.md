## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="nantes-jardin-extraordinaire-what" sources="evidence:nantes-jardin-extraordinaire" -->
Jardin Extraordinaire is a dramatic terraced garden in a former quarry above the Loire, with waterfalls, exotic planting and Barbara Schroeder's Voyage work Les Mistériennes. From May 2026 a 700 m² paddling area gives children a last playful run-around before leaving the city.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** one to one-and-a-half hours including the paddling area.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the paddling zone suits children strongly.
- **Weather fit:** outdoor; swap for the Planetarium on heavy rain days.
- **Booking:** none; free access in summer.
- **Transport:** west bank near Jules Verne; pair the two on departure morning.
- **Safety:** terraces and stairs; supervise paddling and cliff paths.
- **Price / checked date:** free summer access; open from 08:30 (last reviewed 2026-07-24).

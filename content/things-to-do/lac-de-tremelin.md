## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="lac-de-tremelin-what" sources="" -->
Lac de Trémelin is an inland forest lake in the Brocéliande area, used for relaxed freshwater swimming and easy walks. It is a planned stop on the relaxed family route alongside the Vannes/Carnac coast. It is not part of the four audited Deep Research documents, so this page carries no evidence-backed claims: verify facilities, swimming access and water quality yourself via the official tourist offices rather than treating this as reviewed guidance.

## Planning

- **Why it is here:** a calm freshwater option that balances the coastal base for the relaxed route.
- **Before you go:** verify swimming access, lifeguard cover, and water quality against official sources for 2026.
- **Transport:** inland — a car is recommended.
- **Safety:** follow official local guidance for any freshwater swimming.

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="nantes-machines-what" sources="evidence:nantes-machines-overview" -->
Les Machines de l'Île is the headline family stop in Nantes: three ticketed experiences on a former shipyard — the Galerie des Machines (about an hour inside), the Marine Worlds Carrousel (about fifty minutes) and the Grand Éléphant ride (about thirty minutes). The wider Parc des Chantiers, Titan cranes and riverfront sculptures are part of the visit, not just the halls.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day for one or two ticketed experiences; a full day with Carrousel, Galerie, Elephant and shipyard wandering.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the mechanical animals and carousel sea creatures suit a ten-year-old strongly.
- **Weather fit:** mostly under cover in the Galerie and Carrousel; the Elephant is outdoor and weather-dependent.
- **Booking:** priority booking for the Grand Éléphant in August — reserve weeks ahead or arrive at ticket-office opening. Pass Nantes covers Galerie and Carrousel once each but not the Elephant.
- **Transport:** tram to Île de Nantes; Line 1 is partially replaced by buses Jun–Aug 2026 — allow extra transfer time.
- **Safety:** moving machinery and crowds in peak season; hold hands near ride boarding.
- **Price / checked date:** Elephant €12 adult / €10 teen / €8 child 4–12 (last reviewed 2026-07-25); rides can cancel in wind or extreme heat.

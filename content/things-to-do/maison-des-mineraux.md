**Base:** [Crozon / Douarnenez](/bases/crozon-douarnenez)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="maison-des-mineraux-what" sources="evidence:western-maison-des-mineraux" -->
The Maison des Minéraux adds an excellent family layer for a child who likes to understand how landscapes are made, interpreting the peninsula's geology alongside the outdoor trails. It pairs well with the headland walks of Pen-Hir and the Cap de la Chèvre.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 1 to 2 hours; combine with a coastal walk.
- **Age fit:** 6+; ideal for a science-curious 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** partly indoor — a useful stop between outdoor sections or on a wet day.
- **Booking:** check the official site for current opening days before visiting.
- **Transport:** most practical by car; on the Crozon peninsula.
- **Safety:** standard indoor attraction supervision.
- **Price / checked date:** opening days change — re-check on the official site before visiting (last reviewed against the supplied research on 2026-07-02).

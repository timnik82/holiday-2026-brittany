**Base:** [Crozon / Douarnenez](/bases/crozon-douarnenez)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="morgat-what" sources="evidence:western-morgat-beach" -->
Morgat has a golden sandy beach set in an enclosed bay on the Crozon peninsula, where kayaks and paddleboards can be rented. Its shelter makes it the most practical family swimming spot in an otherwise exposed, ocean-facing area.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day or relaxed full day.
- **Age fit:** all ages; shallow play suits a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** the enclosed bay is calmer than the open headlands, but still check sea state on the day.
- **Booking:** none for the beach; book water-sport rentals ahead in August.
- **Transport:** most practical by car; parking fills in peak August.
- **Safety:** standard beach supervision; the sea here is cool even in August.

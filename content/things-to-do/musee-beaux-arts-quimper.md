**Base:** [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="musee-beaux-arts-quimper-what" sources="evidence:western-musee-beaux-arts-quimper,evidence:western-quimper-family-museums" -->
The Musée des Beaux-Arts de Quimper shows about 600 works across roughly 20 rooms, with a strong Breton nineteenth-century and Pont-Aven school collection. Family tours and workshops run regularly; it is the walkable indoor alternative when the old town weather turns.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="musee-beaux-arts-quimper-planning" sources="evidence:western-musee-beaux-arts-quimper" -->
Allow 1.5–2 hours and combine with Saint-Corentin cathedral next door. Tourism sources reported a renovation closure from October 2024 into spring 2026 — confirm August 2026 opening on mbaq.fr before treating this as the wet-day plan.

- **Visit duration:** 1.5 to 2 hours.
- **Age fit:** 8+; stronger if the child likes painting stories than pure play.
- **Weather fit:** fully indoor — the local rain fallback in Quimper reach.
- **Booking:** check mbaq.fr for current opening; workshops may need booking.
- **Transport:** central Quimper, walkable from the historic centre.
- **Safety:** standard museum supervision.
- **Price / checked date:** re-check opening after the 2024–2026 works (last reviewed 2026-07-25).

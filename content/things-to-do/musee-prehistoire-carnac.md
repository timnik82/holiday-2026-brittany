**Base:** [Vannes / Carnac / Morbihan](/bases/vannes-carnac-morbihan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="musee-prehistoire-carnac-what" sources="evidence:southern-musee-prehistoire-carnac,evidence:southern-musee-prehistoire-family" -->
The Musée de Préhistoire de Carnac presents itself as the world's leading megalithism museum, with about 3,000 archaeological objects and a Neolithic focus on the builders of menhirs and dolmens. Family tours, workshops and playful routes make it the natural indoor prologue to the stone fields.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="musee-prehistoire-carnac-planning" sources="evidence:southern-musee-prehistoire-hours,evidence:southern-musee-prehistoire-family" -->
Plan 2–3 hours for the museum, plus separate time for the alignments. In July–August it is typically open daily about 10:00–18:30. Pair a morning indoors with an afternoon among the menhirs; check the children's workshop calendar ahead of an August visit.

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 3 hours indoors; add alignment time separately.
- **Age fit:** 6+; workshops and demos suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** fully indoor — the clearest wet-morning swap in Carnac.
- **Booking:** general entry usually on the day; workshops may need advance booking.
- **Transport:** in Carnac, easy to combine with the Maison des Mégalithes and Grande Plage.
- **Safety:** standard museum supervision.
- **Price / checked date:** hours and workshop slots change — re-check museedecarnac.com (last reviewed 2026-07-25).

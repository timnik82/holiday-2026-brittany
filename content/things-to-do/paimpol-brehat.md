## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="paimpol-brehat-what" sources="evidence:northern-paimpol-brehat-linked-area" -->
West of the granite coast, Paimpol and the Île de Bréhat are treated as a linked day-trip area rather than a seventh base: a working fishing port, a boat crossing to the car-free flower island, and a quieter coastal contrast to Perros-Guirec.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a full day.
- **Age fit:** all ages; walking and cycling on Bréhat suits a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** best in fair weather; the crossing is sea-condition dependent.
- **Booking:** boats to Bréhat run on a seasonal schedule — check times and book ahead in August.
- **Transport:** drive to Paimpol, then the passenger ferry to Bréhat; the island itself is car-free (bikes and walking).
- **Safety:** supervise children on the ferry and around the harbour.

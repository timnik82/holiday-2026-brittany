## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="ploumanach-what" sources="evidence:northern-granit-rose-ploumanach-gr34" -->
The signature walk runs from Ploumanac'h along the GR34 customs path past the pink rocks to the Mean Ruz (Ploumanac'h) lighthouse. Children enjoy scrambling over the granite boulders and reading animal shapes into them; short, manageable sections suit a family that does not want heavy mileage.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day walk, extendable along the GR34.
- **Age fit:** 6+; boulder scrambling is a highlight for a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** best in fair, calm weather; the headland is exposed to wind.
- **Booking:** none — open coastal path.
- **Transport:** easiest by car; parking near Ploumanac'h fills in peak August.
- **Safety:** stay on marked paths near cliff edges; supervise boulder scrambling.

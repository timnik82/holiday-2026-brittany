**Base:** [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="pointe-du-raz-what" sources="evidence:western-pointe-du-raz" -->
The Pointe du Raz, one of Brittany's famous wild headlands, is a practical day trip from a Quimper base — dramatic clifftop walks at the end of the land. It adds a wilderness contrast to the calmer southern Finistère beaches and is best visited in calm, dry weather.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day to a full day.
- **Age fit:** 6+; the cliff paths suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** exposed — best on a calm, dry day; avoid high winds.
- **Booking:** none for the headland paths.
- **Transport:** most practical by car from Quimper.
- **Safety:** unfenced cliff edges in places — keep children on marked paths.

**Base:** [Quimper / South Finistère](/bases/quimper-south-finistere)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="pont-aven-what" sources="evidence:western-pont-aven" -->
Pont-Aven, the riverside painters' town made famous by Gauguin, is one of the small gems within day-trip range of Quimper — galleries, waterside walks, and crêperies rather than beaches. It pairs culture with an easy town stroll.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day; combine with Concarneau or Bénodet.
- **Age fit:** all ages; an easy riverside walk suits a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** mostly outdoor; best in fair weather.
- **Booking:** none for the town; galleries and restaurants may need booking in August.
- **Transport:** within day-trip range of Quimper; a car is recommended.
- **Safety:** standard town supervision.

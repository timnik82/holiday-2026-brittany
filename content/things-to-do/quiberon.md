## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="quiberon-what" sources="evidence:southern-quiberon,evidence:southern-protected-water-beaches" -->
Quiberon is a peninsula with a wild western side of cliffs and waves and softer, sheltered sandy beaches on the east, easy to reach from Carnac or Vannes. The same peninsula shelters Carnac's beaches from the heavy swell, keeping the gulf water calm and safe for children.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day to a full day.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the sheltered beaches suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** the wild western coast is best in calm weather; the eastern beaches are more forgiving.
- **Booking:** none for the peninsula; ferries to offshore islands from Quiberon may need booking in August.
- **Transport:** a short drive from Carnac; a car is helpful.
- **Safety:** supervise children on the exposed western cliffs and keep to marked paths.

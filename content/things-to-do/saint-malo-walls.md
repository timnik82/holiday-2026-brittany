**Base:** [Saint-Malo / Dinan](/bases/saint-malo-dinan)

## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="saint-malo-walls-what" sources="evidence:northern-saint-malo-ramparts-walk" -->
A walk of roughly 2 km along the top of Saint-Malo's ramparts loops the old corsair city (Intra-Muros), with views over the English Channel, the fort islands, and the beaches. It is the natural spine of a visit and works in most weather.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="saint-malo-walls-planning" sources="evidence:northern-saint-malo-parking-shuttle,evidence:northern-saint-malo-ramparts-walk" -->
Intra-Muros street parking is expensive and full in August. Prefer an intercept option instead:

- **Intercept car park:** ~800 spaces, about €3.30/24h, with a free shuttle to the old town every ~20 minutes in summer.
- **EFFIA parks:** along the walls — useful if the intercept lot is full.
- **Rocabey:** free, but a 15–20 minute walk into Intra-Muros.

- **Visit duration:** half a day, longer if you add the tidal islands.
- **Age fit:** 6+; the wall-top path is the highlight for a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** works in most conditions; the ramparts are exposed to wind.
- **Booking:** none for the walls themselves; town museums and fort islands vary.
- **Transport:** walk from anywhere in Saint-Malo once you are parked or shuttled in.
- **Safety:** the causeways to Grand Bé and Petit Bé are tidal — check the day's tide table and start back while the path is dry.

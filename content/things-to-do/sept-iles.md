## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="sept-iles-what" sources="evidence:northern-granit-rose-sept-iles" -->
From Perros-Guirec, boat excursions run to the Sept-Îles archipelago, a seabird reserve that is one of France's largest Atlantic puffin sites. The trip combines wildlife viewing with a coastal cruise. Landings are restricted, so most visits are boat-based viewing.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 3 hours.
- **Age fit:** 5+; good for a wildlife-curious 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** trips are weather-dependent — check sea conditions on the day.
- **Booking:** book ahead in August; boats fill in peak season.
- **Transport:** departs from Perros-Guirec harbour.
- **Safety:** supervise children on board; bring layers for wind and spray.

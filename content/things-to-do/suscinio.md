## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="suscinio-what" sources="evidence:southern-suscinio" -->
The Château de Suscinio, a ducal Brittany castle on the Rhuys peninsula, runs interactive knight quests, night light shows and costumed tours for children, pairing a real medieval fortress with a beach walk nearby.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** 2 to 4 hours; combine with a Rhuys beach.
- **Age fit:** 6+; the quests and castle suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** the castle interior offers cover; shows may be weather-dependent.
- **Booking:** check the official site for show days and tour times; book ahead in August.
- **Transport:** most practical by car, on the Rhuys peninsula.
- **Safety:** standard castle supervision near moats and stairs.
- **Price / checked date:** show and tour days change — re-check on the official site before visiting (last reviewed against the supplied research on 2026-07-02).

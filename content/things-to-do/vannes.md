## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="vannes-what" sources="evidence:southern-vannes-town,evidence:southern-vannes-beaches-conleau" -->
Vannes is a handsome medieval town with a port and walls, well placed for the Morbihan gulf and the Rhuys peninsula, and often used as a walkable urban base for radial trips to beaches and castles. Its market is one of the region's best city markets, and Plage de Conleau is about 5 km away on bus line 3 when you want a short gulf swim.

## Planning

<!-- paragraph id="vannes-planning" sources="evidence:southern-vannes-town,evidence:southern-vannes-beaches-conleau" -->
Spread the old town across the stay rather than rushing it in one block: walls and port one evening, market morning another day, Conleau when the weather is fair but energy is low. The town covers a wet morning better than the open gulf.

<!-- paragraph id="vannes-food" sources="evidence:southern-food,evidence:southern-food-vannes-practical" -->
Crêperies and galetteries are the cheapest authentic family meals; oysters and simple seafood plates fill the gaps. Book nothing elaborate for every night — market days and a crêperie evening keep the food plan light.

- **Visit duration:** a half-day to a full day, or spread across a stay.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the walls, port and market suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** the old town and market cover a wet morning; the ramparts are best in fair weather.
- **Booking:** none for the town; market days and restaurants vary — check locally.
- **Transport:** walkable and on the Nantes–Vannes rail line; bus line 3 for Conleau.
- **Safety:** standard town supervision.

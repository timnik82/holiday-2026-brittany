## What it is

<!-- paragraph id="vannes-what" sources="evidence:southern-vannes-town" -->
Vannes is a handsome medieval town with a port and walls, well placed for the Morbihan gulf and the Rhuys peninsula, and often used as a walkable urban base for radial trips to beaches and castles. Its market is one of the region's best city markets, and a weekly family route around the gulf is officially recommended.

## Planning

- **Visit duration:** a half-day to a full day, or spread across a stay.
- **Age fit:** all ages; the walls, port and market suit a 9 to 10-year-old.
- **Weather fit:** the old town and market cover a wet morning; the ramparts are best in fair weather.
- **Booking:** none for the town; market days and restaurants vary — check locally.
- **Transport:** the city is walkable and on the Nantes–Vannes rail line.
- **Safety:** standard town supervision.

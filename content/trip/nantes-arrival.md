## Why we are here

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-overview" sources="evidence:nantes-itinerary-overview,evidence:nantes-must-do-core" -->
Nantes is the gateway, not a Brittany base — three nights to recover from the flight and hit the one city the rest of the trip does not cover. The plan is three themed days: mechanical fantasy on the island, castle and garden on Saturday, Jules Verne and the west bank before moving on. Le Voyage à Nantes runs through the stay, so public art threads through every walk.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-strategy" sources="evidence:nantes-stay-strategy" -->
Stay central near Commerce and Bouffay so tram hops and walking stay realistic. One headline child-magnet — Les Machines de l'Île — anchors the stay; everything else layers around it rather than trying to clear every museum.

## Getting here

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-arrival" sources="evidence:nantes-arrival-shuttle,evidence:nantes-primary-gateway-from-portugal" -->
We land at Nantes Atlantique on 6 August at 20:05. The airport shuttle runs every twenty minutes in summer with the last departure at midnight; allow margin for baggage. A car is optional in the city centre — weekend transport is free on 8–9 August, though Line 1 tram works Jun–Aug 2026 mean bus replacements toward the island.

## Day by day

### Thursday 6 August — arrival

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-day-0" sources="evidence:nantes-arrival-evening,evidence:nantes-arrival-shuttle" -->
Shuttle to the hotel and an easy dinner only — no evening programme. An optional stroll to the Water Mirror opposite the castle is enough if anyone still has energy.

### Friday 7 August — Machines and the island

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-day-1" sources="evidence:nantes-day-7-machines,evidence:nantes-machines-elephant-booking,evidence:nantes-le-voyage-hab-galerie" -->
Headline day at [Les Machines de l'Île](/things-to-do/les-machines-de-l-ile): book the Grand Éléphant ahead if possible, allow two and a half to three and a half hours, then wander Parc des Chantiers. Add the free INTERSTELLAR show at HAB Galerie on the same island if mood allows. Wet-weather swap: Illusion Nantes (confirm price at checkout) or stay longer in the Galerie.

### Saturday 8 August — castle, market and garden

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-day-2" sources="evidence:nantes-day-8-castle-garden,evidence:nantes-food-talensac,evidence:nantes-transport-weekend-free" -->
Optional early stop at Talensac market for picnic supplies, then [Château des ducs de Bretagne](/things-to-do/chateau-des-ducs-nantes) in the morning and [Jardin des Plantes](/things-to-do/jardin-des-plantes-nantes) for a slow afternoon. Follow the [green line](/things-to-do/le-voyage-a-nantes) through Bouffay in the evening. City transport is free all day — lunch at a Bouffay crêperie fits naturally here.

### Sunday 9 August — west bank, then Vannes

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-day-3" sources="evidence:nantes-day-9-jules-verne" -->
Half-day before the train: [Musée Jules Verne and Planetarium](/things-to-do/jules-verne-planetarium) at opening, then [Jardin Extraordinaire](/things-to-do/jardin-extraordinaire) if time allows. Insert the Planetarium only if a session fits the departure window.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-moving-on" sources="evidence:nantes-day-9-departure,evidence:southern-logistics-nantes" -->
Check out and take the train to Vannes — about one hour twelve minutes, one of the softest transfers on the trip. Re-check SNCF schedules before travel day.

## Pace and weather

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-pace" sources="evidence:nantes-pace-balance" -->
Each day holds two or three anchors with slack between them. Drop a tier rather than compressing when the child tires.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-arrival-rainy" sources="evidence:nantes-rainy-fallbacks,evidence:nantes-natural-history-closed,evidence:nantes-musee-arts-summer,evidence:nantes-illusion-price-conflict" -->
Rain or heat pivots: Planetarium, Musée d'arts (summer hours — permanent collection closed Tuesdays), Dobrée Museum, Illusion Nantes or HAB Galerie. Do not route to the Natural History Museum main building — closed until 2029.

## Why we are here

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-departure-overview" sources="evidence:nantes-itinerary-overview" -->
One night in Nantes after four days in Saint-Malo and Dinan — close enough to the airport for an unhurried departure without another full city programme. The booked stay is a buffer, not a second three-day itinerary.

## Getting here

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-departure-arrival" sources="evidence:southern-logistics-nantes" -->
We return from Brittany by train or car depending on how the last stay ends; Nantes is the natural gateway for the flight home. Central hotels keep the shuttle simple on departure morning.

## Day by day

### Saturday 22 August — check-in and a quiet evening

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-departure-day-1" sources="evidence:nantes-stay-strategy,evidence:nantes-le-voyage-green-line" -->
Settle in and take a relaxed [green-line](/things-to-do/le-voyage-a-nantes) stroll through Bouffay or a crêperie dinner — nothing ambitious after the drive or train from the north.

### Sunday 23 August — flight home

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-departure-day-2" sources="evidence:nantes-arrival-shuttle" -->
Short morning, then the airport shuttle — every twenty minutes in summer, about twenty to twenty-five minutes to the terminal. Re-check the flight time and allow margin for Sunday traffic.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-departure-shuttle" sources="evidence:nantes-arrival-shuttle" -->
The shuttle costs €10 unless covered by an activated Pass Nantes return. Last airport departure is at midnight if plans change.

## If time allows

<!-- paragraph id="stay-nantes-departure-revisit" sources="evidence:nantes-must-do-core" -->
Anyone with energy on Saturday evening could revisit [Les Machines de l'Île](/things-to-do/les-machines-de-l-ile) or the [castle ramparts](/things-to-do/chateau-des-ducs-nantes) — both are nearby — but the default plan is rest before the flight.

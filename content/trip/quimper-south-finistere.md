## Why we are here

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-overview" sources="evidence:western-quimper-culture-base" -->
Quimper is a handsome historic city with the cathedral core of Saint-Corentin, half-timbered houses and a faïence quarter — described in the research as much more charming than Brest and as the cultural capital of Brittany. It is not a beach on the doorstep. Its value on this trip is as a hub: Locronan, Concarneau, Pont-Aven, Fouesnant and Bénodet all sit close by.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-shape" sources="evidence:routes-nature-sequencing,evidence:routes-nature-stay-lengths" -->
Four nights is what the sources allocate to this region, and one of them describes precisely the shape we are using: roughly four days based around Quimper, with a Crozon peninsula day and an Océanopolis day taken as trips out from the city rather than as separate bases. That is the arrangement below.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-pairing" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-quimper-extension" -->
One honest caveat. No single source chains Quimper with Saint-Malo and Dinan into one itinerary — the research normally pairs Quimper with the wild-coast material via Crozon. Our route does chain them, so the link between this stay and the next is our own synthesis across base-level evidence, not a plan any source endorsed.

## Getting here and getting around

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-car" sources="evidence:routes-car-required-flexible,evidence:western-crozon-logistics-car" -->
This is the stay where a car stops being a convenience. The city centre is walkable, but every day below is a drive, and the Crozon viewpoints in particular are dispersed along local roads that trains and BreizhGo buses do not reliably cover. Public transport would cut this stay down to the city itself.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-rail" sources="evidence:western-quimper-logistics" -->
For reference, Quimper is reachable by rail via Nantes in about 2 h 23 min, and its own modest airport is a 17-minute drive from the centre with a direct Paris link. We arrive overland from Vannes instead; the sources do not cover that leg directly.

## Day by day

### Friday 14 August — arrival and the old city

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-day-1" sources="evidence:western-quimper-family-museums" -->
Arrive from Vannes and walk [Quimper](/things-to-do/quimper) itself. The city works better for a child than it first appears: the Musée des Beaux-Arts runs family formats such as livrets-jeux, the faïence museum sits nearby, and a tourist Petit Train loops the medieval centre.

### Saturday 15 August — Locronan and the Pointe du Raz

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-day-2" sources="evidence:western-locronan,evidence:western-pointe-du-raz" -->
West to [Locronan](/things-to-do/locronan), one of France's most-beautiful granite villages and only 20 to 30 minutes away, then further out to the [Pointe du Raz](/things-to-do/pointe-du-raz) — a wild end-of-the-land headland. Wind layers and proper shoes; this is the most exposed day of the stay.

### Sunday 16 August — the Crozon peninsula

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-day-3" sources="evidence:routes-nature-sequencing,evidence:western-crozon-wild-coast" -->
North to [Crozon](/bases/crozon-douarnenez) for the day the sources build into a Quimper base: the clifftop heath and rocks of [Pen-Hir](/things-to-do/crozon-pen-hir), the wild paths of the Cap de la Chèvre, and the strongest end-of-the-land feel of the trip.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-morgat" sources="evidence:western-morgat-beach,evidence:western-maison-des-mineraux" -->
The same day has two softer options attached. [Morgat](/things-to-do/morgat) is a golden beach in an enclosed bay — the most sheltered swimming on the peninsula, with kayaks and paddleboards to rent. If the weather turns, the [Maison des Minéraux](/things-to-do/maison-des-mineraux) interprets the peninsula's geology for a child who likes to know how a landscape was made.

### Monday 17 August — Concarneau, Pont-Aven or Bénodet

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-day-4" sources="evidence:western-concarneau-filets-bleus,evidence:western-pont-aven,evidence:western-benodet-glenan" -->
East instead of west, and pick by weather. [Concarneau](/things-to-do/concarneau) has its walled Ville Close and, in August, the Festival des Filets Bleus. [Pont-Aven](/things-to-do/pont-aven) is the riverside painters' town. [Bénodet](/things-to-do/benodet) is the family beach, with boats out to the Glénan islands.

### Tuesday 18 August — north to Saint-Malo and Dinan

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-moving-on" sources="" -->
The longest move of the trip, right across Brittany from the south-west to the north coast. Nothing is planned for the arrival afternoon beyond settling in. The research corpus does not cover this crossing, so allow more time than a map suggests.

## If it rains, or if there is a spare day

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-oceanopolis" sources="evidence:western-brest-oceanopolis,evidence:routes-nature-overview" -->
[Océanopolis](/things-to-do/oceanopolis) at Brest is the region's major ocean-science aquarium and the one indoor attraction big enough to hold a whole wet day. The sources treat it as a day trip from a Quimper base rather than a reason to move north.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-quimper-haliotika" sources="evidence:western-haliotika" -->
Closer to hand, the [Haliotika fishing discovery centre](/things-to-do/haliotika) at Le Guilvinec covers the working side of the coast, and Quimper's own museums absorb a morning without a drive.

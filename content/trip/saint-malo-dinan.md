## Why we are here

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-overview" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-overview" -->
This is the history end of the trip. A walled corsair city and a separate medieval river town, with tidal island forts, family beaches and a strong aquarium in the same place, and Mont-Saint-Michel, Cap Fréhel and Cancale stacked on top as day trips. The research describes it as the base that lands best when a child likes castles, knights and forts.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-shape" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-sequencing,evidence:routes-cultural-stay-lengths" -->
The sources allocate about four nights to Saint-Malo and two or three to Dinan, sequencing the coast first and the medieval town last. They also keep the two together, treating the hop between them as a short transfer rather than a travel day. We take the four Saint-Malo nights and visit Dinan from them, so there is no second check-in at the end of the trip.

## Getting here and getting around

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-arrival" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-transfers" -->
We arrive overland from Quimper rather than by the route the sources describe, which enters from Nantes by train in about 2 h 24 min. What does carry over is the local geometry: Dinan is roughly 40 minutes away by car, Mont-Saint-Michel about an hour. Times are seasonal and scheduled, not door-to-door promises.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-car" sources="evidence:routes-car-required-flexible" -->
A car is recommended rather than essential. Saint-Malo's old town is walkable and rail-connected, but Cap Fréhel, Fort La Latte, Mont-Saint-Michel and most of the beaches are far easier with one — and an organised tour or a taxi covers the odd day if it comes to that.

## Day by day

### Tuesday 18 August — arrival and a first loop of the walls

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-day-1" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-stay-lengths" -->
After the long drive north, nothing ambitious. A first walk of the [ramparts and Intra-Muros](/things-to-do/saint-malo-walls) in the evening and a crêperie for dinner is the whole plan.

### Wednesday 19 August — ramparts, Bon-Secours and the fort islands

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-day-2" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-stay-lengths" -->
Morning on the ramparts and [Plage du Bon-Secours](/things-to-do/bon-secours), then — if the tide is out — the causeway walk to Grand Bé and Petit Bé and their 17th-century fort. Check the day's tide table first and start back while the causeway is still dry.

### Thursday 20 August — Mont-Saint-Michel

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-day-3" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-transfers,evidence:routes-cultural-stay-lengths" -->
A day trip to [Mont-Saint-Michel](/things-to-do/mont-saint-michel), about an hour away. Go early to beat both the crowds and the causeway traffic. This is the day the car most obviously pays for itself; without one it becomes an organised bus tour from Saint-Malo.

### Friday 21 August — Dinan

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-day-4" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-sequencing,evidence:routes-cultural-transfers" -->
Inland to [Dinan](/things-to-do/dinan) — the 14th-century castle, the half-timbered streets, the steep cobbled rue du Jerzual down to the port, and a stretch of the Rance river. In summer a Rance river cruise is an alternative to walking back up the hill. Thursday is market day, so we miss it by one.

### Saturday 22 August — south to Nantes

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-moving-on" sources="" -->
Check out and travel back to Nantes for the last night before the flight home. Keep the morning unhurried; the Nantes evening is a separate stay with its own short programme.

## Alternatives and wet weather

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-alternatives" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-stay-lengths" -->
Two days above can be swapped without losing much. [Cap Fréhel and Fort La Latte](/things-to-do/cap-frehel-fort-la-latte) offer clifftop heath, sea views and a headland castle, most practical by car; [Cancale](/things-to-do/cancale) trades the cliffs for oysters at the harbour stalls and a short coastal walk.

<!-- paragraph id="stay-saint-malo-rain" sources="evidence:routes-cultural-stay-lengths" -->
The [Grand Aquarium](/things-to-do/grand-aquarium) is the area's main rainy-day anchor and can absorb a full day on its own; paired with the pirate-themed Labyrinthe du Corsaire it covers the worst weather this stay is likely to produce.
